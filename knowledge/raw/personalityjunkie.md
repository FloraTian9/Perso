## 主体（按人格）

### 通用页证据

#### 通用：8个认知功能定义

- 来源 URL：https://personalityjunkie.com/functions-ni-ti-fi-si-ne-te-fe-se/
- 覆盖问题：A1, A2, A3, A4, A6

**功能分类框架：**

*内倾 vs 外倾（Concealed vs Expressed）*
- Introverted (I) functions: Ni, Si, Ti, Fi — directed inwardly
- Extraverted (E) functions: Ne, Se, Te, Fe — directed outwardly
- Introverts' dominant function is introverted and not readily discerned by others; what people typically encounter is the extraverted auxiliary function

*判断 vs 感知（Judging vs Perceiving）*
- Judging functions: make decisions and draw conclusions; involve desire to control, predict, order or shape the course of things
- Perceiving functions: take in or retrieve information; observing, sensing, recalling from memory

*自主 vs 共享（Autonomous vs Shared）*
- Fi and Ti (used by FPs and TPs): autonomous — reasons, values, and methods derived from within; judgments may seem idiosyncratic or unorthodox
- Fe and Te (used by FJs and TJs): oriented to shared or collective norms — Fe aligns with social customs/manners, Te aligns with external rational standards
- "Ti being more rebellious or 'freethinking' in T matters, Fi in F matters"

*收敛 vs 发散（Convergent vs Divergent）*
- Ni and Si: intensive/convergent — winnow down perceptions to achieve coherent, convergent understanding
- Ne and Se: extensive/divergent — spread across variety of ideas/experiences

**Ni（内倾直觉）— "Seeing"**
- Used as dominant or auxiliary by Intuitive Judging (NJ) types
- Strong visual element to cognition — INJs think in images as much as words
- Ni "seeing" refers to both literal envisioning and understanding
- Takes a bird's eye view or looks beneath the surface of things
- More concerned with forming holistic impression than attending to specific details
- Affords intimate relationship with workings of the subconscious mind
- INFJs/INTJs commonly viewed as profound, insightful, sometimes psychic

**Ne（外倾直觉）— "Ideating"**
- Used as dominant or auxiliary by Intuitive Perceiving (NP) types
- Divergent form of intuition — specializes in generating lots of ideas, connections, and possibilities
- The verbal expression of Ne often resembles brainstorming aloud
- NPs may not always seem to "have a point" as they meander from one idea to the next
- To other types, Ne can seem random, distractible, quirky, or flighty

**Fi（内倾情感）— "Valuing"**
- Used as dominant or auxiliary by Feeling Perceiving (FP) types
- Directed inwardly — navigates and manages personal feelings and values on largely independent basis
- FPs use personal values as a compass for navigating life
- Will go against cultural grain if something doesn't sit right with them
- FPs extend freedom to others — less inclined to impose their values on others
- Expresses through various indirect channels (art, creativity, nature connection)

**Fe（外倾情感）— "Connecting"**
- Used as dominant or auxiliary by Feeling Judging (FJ) types
- Most emotionally expressive and communicative of all types
- Less emotionally autonomous than Fi; calibrates to social context
- Excels at reading and mirroring others' emotions
- Foremost concern: establishing emotional rapport with others
- FJs notice and meet needs of others; want everyone getting along, treated humanely and hospitably

**Ti（内倾思考）— "Reasoning"**
- Used as dominant or auxiliary by Thinking Perceiving (TP) types
- Innate proclivity for logical reasoning — spatial, mathematical, or otherwise
- "Do-It-Yourself" orientation; resistant to accepting others' explanations as-is
- Philosophy: haven for "freethinkers," scrutinizing assumptions of widely accepted beliefs
- Ti is equally relevant for practical (S) problems as abstract (N) ones; STPs focus former, NTPs latter

**Te（外倾思考）— "Structuring"**
- Used as dominant or auxiliary by Thinking Judging (TJ) types
- Outward expression of rational judgments and assertions; TJs "think out loud"
- Firm and frank expression — "straight shooters"
- Aims to rationally understand external systems/structures, diagnose shortcomings, make them more efficient
- Unlike TPs, TJs prefer not to improvise; employ proven ways of doing things
- NTJs will jettison/revise existing standards when necessary (product of intuition more than Te)
- Proficiency with planning, organizing, mapping, quantification

**Si（内倾感觉）— "Stabilizing"**
- Used as dominant or auxiliary by Sensing Judging (SJ) types
- Growing attachment to routine, familiar, and expected
- Repetition: the more SJs repeat something, the more likeable it becomes
- Conservative approach to beliefs and worldview
- Familiarity confers safety and security
- Perpetuating the past into the present and future — knowing what to expect

**Se（外倾感觉）— "Experiencing"**
- Used as dominant or auxiliary by Sensing Perceiving (SP) types
- Mediates relationship to physical surroundings
- Relish novel sensory experiences, material pleasures, thrill of action
- Aesthetic preferences often reflect what's popular or trendy
- Some SPs develop expensive taste (food, wine, cars, accommodations)
- Excel at hands-on work: cooking, carpentry, auto repair, surgery, emergency response, personal training

#### 通用：Ni（内倾直觉）深度解析

- 来源 URL：https://personalityjunkie.com/10/introverted-intuition-ni/
- 覆盖问题：A1, A2, A3, A4, A9

**Ni 的工作机制：**
- Does much of its work subconsciously — can seem to have "a certain magical quality"
- INJs often viewed as having "some degree of psychic or prophetic abilities"
- Highly sensitive inferior Se gathers "copious amounts of sensory information from the outside world, including subtleties that other personality types tend to miss"
- Ni then subconsciously processes this data "like assembling pieces of a puzzle"
- Once finished, generates an impression that seems to come "out of nowhere" — but actually from synthesis of sensory data + psyche information

**视觉特征（Visual Nature）：**
- INJs "often report a strong visual element to their Introverted Intuition"
- "Think by way of images rather than words"
- Intuitions manifest as "symbols, images, dreams, or patterns"
- Jung characterized Ni type as "a dreamer, artist, or seer"
- Vision-related terms invariably used: foresight, insight, seer, visionary

**美学敏感性：**
- Many INJs "highly sensitive to beauty — visual, metaphorical, or otherwise"
- INJs "the most otherworldly and abstract types, are often perplexed by their felt need to create beauty and ensconce themselves in beautiful surroundings"
- INFJ vs INFP difference: "INFJs tend to have far more refined, sophisticated, and exquisite tastes than INFPs, for whom sensing is introverted (Si)"

**大局观（Big Picture Thinking）：**
- "Of all types, INJs are those most concerned with the 'big picture'"
- Ni is "the most abstract and forward-looking of all functions"
- "Comprehensive and holistic" — visions manifest as comprehensive wholes
- INJs "often feel more like recipients than they do creators of their ingenious ideas"
- Strong sense of foresight: "feel they have been granted a sneak preview of the future"

**收敛性解决方案（Convergent Solutions）：**
- While not technically a judging function, Ni "often functions in a convergent fashion, providing elegant answers and solutions to complex problems"
- Solution arrives through "a single flash of insight — an 'aha!' moment"
- May occur while dreaming or awake, "tends to come suddenly and all at once, like an unexpected gift"
- Nietzsche's description: "Something profoundly convulsive...suddenly becomes visible and audible with indescribable definiteness and exactness...an ecstasy...Everything occurs without volition, as if an eruption of freedom, independence, power, and divinity"

**确信感（Conviction）：**
- Powerful means by which Ni reveals insights "associated with a gut sense of conviction and certainty"
- "At a deep intuitive level, INJs experience their intuitions as true and trustworthy"
- But must work to flesh it out — articulate and illustrate to render it "accessible and useful to others"
- Auxiliary function (Fe or Te) helps "unpack their vision, sort of like decompressing a computer file"
- Process can be "difficult and painstaking, sometimes taking longer than birthing the vision itself"

**处理悖论（Handling Paradoxes）：**
- Ni "can also be an effective tool for handling paradoxical or contradictory evidence"
- Rational mind: two contradictory assertions cannot simultaneously coexist
- But unconscious, through innate creativity, "adept at reconciling opposites and transcending paradoxes"
- Example: INFJ claiming "knowledge is at once relative and absolute, subjective and objective, temporal and eternal"
- Through lens of Ni, "ostensible paradoxes may become more readily intelligible and explainable"

#### 通用：功能栈理论

- 来源 URL：https://personalityjunkie.com/functional-stack-type-dynamics-theory/
- 覆盖问题：A5, A6

**四个栈位：**

*主导功能（Dominant）*
- "The type's signature strength" — most differentiated and conscious function
- When engaged in activities utilizing this function: "feel alert and alive, doing what we were born to do"

*辅助功能（Auxiliary）*
- "Sidekick to the dominant function" — second most developed
- Well-developed and useful; supports the dominant

*第三功能（Tertiary）*
- "Relatively unconscious / undifferentiated" — third in hierarchy
- Less reliable; can be used but often in immature ways

*劣势功能（Inferior）*
- "Least conscious / accessible" — weakest function in the stack
- Despite being least conscious, plays important role in personality dynamics
- Particularly influential in each type's psychological drama

**关键规则（确定功能栈）：**
1. 外倾型展露其主导功能（外倾）；内倾型隐藏主导、展露辅助功能
2. J 型在顶位使用外倾判断功能（Te/Fe）；P 型在顶位使用外倾感知功能（Se/Ne）
3. 功能配对为对立组：Ti-Fe, Te-Fi, Ne-Si, Ni-Se

### INFJ

- 来源 URL：https://personalityjunkie.com/the-infj/
- 功能栈：Ni（主导）- Fe（辅助）- Ti（第三）- Se（劣势）
- 覆盖问题：B1, B2, B3, B4, B8, B9, B12

**核心特征：**
- "INFJs are 'old souls'" — feel wiser than their chronological age
- Experience "a perpetual sense of deja vu"
- Often feel like aliens in the world
- Dual vision: see public personas while simultaneously perceiving deeper dimensions through Ni, revealing "hidden motives and intentions"
- Inner world "well described as playful, imaginative, colorful, mischievous, and daring" despite appearing serious externally

**信息处理：**
- Ni synthesizes sensory data subconsciously, constructing impressions and visions
- Think "through images rather than words" — intuitions manifest as "symbols, images, dreams, or patterns"
- Excel at "reconciling opposites" through visual-symbolic thinking
- Access problem-solving approaches unavailable through purely verbal reasoning
- Often require verbalization to fully clarify intuitions: "do not fully understand the nature of an Ni insight until given the opportunity to verbalize it"
- Prefer extended monologues over dialogical exchanges

**核心驱动力：**
- Ni: convergent insight, pattern recognition, foresight into underlying dynamics
- Fe: establishing emotional rapport, maintaining relational harmony, helping others
- Fundamental conflict: honoring personal convictions (Ni) vs. maintaining relational harmony (Fe), often defaulting to people-pleasing despite inward regret

**情绪光谱：**
- With strangers: appear "consistently cheery" while cultivating favorable atmospheres
- With intimates: become "more open and direct," sometimes appearing "moody, pessimistic, discontented, or restless"
- Fe directed outwardly: "less equipped to manage their emotions independently"
- Inclined toward seeking "emotional support, empathy, and reassurance"
- Psychological risk: depression from feeling "chronically unheard, useless, or misunderstood" combined with sense of isolation

**劣势功能（Se）压力反应：**
- As "highly sensitive persons," experience environmental overstimulation subtly and unconsciously
- "Tenuous relationship with the physical environment" — sometimes unaware of sensory exhaustion until "crash"
- Pursue "perfect materialization (Se) of their ideals (Ni)" — willing to "sacrifice everything, even health"
- Perfectionism: harsh self-judgment; if cannot "perfectly embody moral ideal" cannot expect others to

**决策方式：**
- "When it comes to decision-making, INFJs are wise to listen primarily to their own inner voice"
- Ni serves as "their best and most reliable compass"

**发展阶段：**
- Phase I (Childhood): Ni-Fe function pair dominates; may appear overly opinionated from without
- Phase II (Adolescence-30s): Se inferior enters the picture; Ti tertiary begins cross-checking Ni-Fe judgments
- Phase III (30s+): Integration of tertiary (Ti) and inferior (Se); path toward wholeness

**人口统计：** 仅占总人口1-2%，男女比例相对均衡

### INTJ

- 来源 URL：https://personalityjunkie.com/the-intj/
- 功能栈：Ni（主导）- Te（辅助）- Fi（第三）- Se（劣势）
- 覆盖问题：B1, B2, B3, B4, B8, B9

**核心特征：**
- Many INTJs seem to have "photographic memories" — can recall nearly anything exposed to
- Can recite lines from movies watched only once
- "Dreamlike" existence focused on internal mental models rather than external details
- Operate through impressionistic perception, seeing interconnections others miss
- Colleagues may perceive them as "quirky, awkward, nerdy, or oblivious" due to absorption in abstract thought
- Despite introverted status, "surprisingly talkative" on subjects of interest

**信息处理：**
- Ni operates as "thinking without thinking" — develop insights that "seem to emerge ex nihilo"
- Use Te to articulate Ni findings logically afterward
- Visual thinking predominates — utilize "images more than words"
- Vast stores of factual knowledge (Te): naturally inclined to share what they know

**核心驱动力：**
- Recognizing their Ni powers of insight + Te knowledge stores → inclined to lecture/teach
- "dispensing ideas or information is one of the easiest ways for INTJs to engage with others"
- Because Te is extraverted, others commonly (often wrongly) assume them to be characteristically serious

**情绪与人际：**
- "Intensive emotional investments in a limited number of individuals" rather than broad social networks
- Show loyalty to family and close friends
- Emotionally maintain stoic exterior; inner Fi feelings operate quietly
- Some INTJs begin every day with comedy (online memes) — not always as serious as they appear

**劣势功能（Se）压力反应：**
- Phase II: inferior Se asserts itself more prominently
- May show greater interest in novel aesthetic/sensory experiences
- INTJ children often leery of new experiences; with time, gradually open to S novelties
- Under Se inferior's spell: can make questionable career or relationship decisions ("opposites attract" / inferior function attraction phenomenon)
- Perfectionism: integrating idealistic visions with reality; sometimes obsessive persistence on minutiae

**发展阶段：**
- Phase I (Childhood): Ni dominant emerges; amass information for Ni to process; crystallizing worldview
- Phase II (Adolescence-30s): Se inferior asserts itself; siren call can lead to questionable decisions; counterbalanced by developing Te
- Phase III (30s+): Wise up to tricks of inferior; discover sustainable ways of integrating functions; flow states in Ni-Te activities

### INTP

- 来源 URL：https://personalityjunkie.com/the-intp/
- 功能栈：Ti（主导）- Ne（辅助）- Si（第三）- Fe（劣势）
- 覆盖问题：B1, B2, B3, B4, B8, B9, B11

**核心特征：**
- "The most independent and philosophical of the 16 types"
- "Deep need for personal autonomy and freedom of thought"
- "Insatiable appetite for ideation and theorizing"
- Explore philosophy, religion, and metaphysical concepts
- Prioritize ideas over facts; constantly examine "background of their own thoughts"
- Reluctance to display rational dimension; competence goes "unnoticed by others"
- Anxiety and self-consciousness; avoid "direct eye contact"

**信息处理：**
- Ti: apply logic and reason for understanding situations, systems, or problems
- Bring structure and order to inner world; strong sense of inner control
- Excel at identifying "inconsistencies or logical shortcomings — assert what is not true"
- Start with a given (Ti) then use Ne to explore various connections and possibilities
- Trial-and-error approach to building theories
- Perpetual doubt: "at the very moment they feel good about a given conclusion, Ne steps in and causes them to start doubting it again"

**对话风格：**
- When engaged: "stimulating conversationalists" who "easily connect one topic to another"
- Quickly disengage from "protracted small talk" and "zone out"
- Prefer discussing ideas to "mundane details of people's lives"
- Ne expression: brainstorming aloud; "may not always seem to 'have a point'"

**情绪与人际：**
- Fe inferior: feelings "seem to have a mind of their own, often coming and going as they please"
- Appear "outwardly warm, but inwardly cold or calculating"
- Oscillation between complete independence and longing for connection
- "Lifelong challenge" balancing "Ti lopsidedness" with relational needs
- Tug-of-war between Ti (independence) and Fe (relationships)

**劣势功能（Fe）压力反应：**
- Under grip: emotions flood unexpectedly; may feel overwhelmed by feelings they cannot explain
- May lash out emotionally or dramatically in ways inconsistent with normal Ti reserve
- Difficulty making decisions due to perpetual doubt
- Oscillating between "don't need other people at all" and sensing "something important is missing"

**发展阶段：**
- Phase I (Childhood): Ti emergence focused on one or two specific pursuits
- Phase II (Adolescence-30s): Fe inferior enters picture; Ne develops; philosophical awakening; extended identity exploration
- Phase III (30s+): Greater balance between Ti and Fe; natural integration if developmental trajectory succeeds

### INFP

- 来源 URL：https://personalityjunkie.com/the-infp/
- 功能栈：Fi（主导）- Ne（辅助）- Si（第三）- Te（劣势）
- 覆盖问题：B1, B2, B3, B4, B8, B9

**核心特征：**
- "Creative, quirky, humane, and individualistic"
- Strong idealism: "rarely content with 'what is,' preferring to focus on 'what could be'"
- Among "the most open-minded (and open-hearted) of the personality types"
- Jung characterized them as "mostly silent, inaccessible, hard to understand" yet internally possess "abundant" inner emotional landscape
- "Good listeners as well as facilitators of conversation"

**核心驱动力：**
- "Seek a life of passion and intensity"
- Want to understand "what it means to be fully alive"
- Gravitate toward experiences that "arouse or intensify feelings of passion, inspiration, or meaning"
- Material wealth holds minimal importance: "Money is valued only to the extent it furnishes time and freedom to explore deepest passions"

**信息处理：**
- Fi: process emotions and experiences on largely independent basis
- Develop identity through internal reflection: "With each new feeling, experience, or idea evaluated, sense of self becomes clearer"
- Ne drives exploration across ideas and possibilities
- When receiving information: "gather information" and "scan for new patterns, associations, and possibilities"
- Ne tension: "at the very moment they feel confident about a given conclusion, Ne finds a way to inject doubt and uncertainty"

**决策方式：**
- Fi-first: judge internally before perceiving externally (opposite of ENFPs who Ne-first)
- Authentic interest in understanding others "in non-threatening, non-judgmental way"
- Enjoys "asking probing questions"

**情绪光谱：**
- Despite inner emotional richness, often appear reserved
- Strong, mostly silent emotional interior
- Creative, imaginative, individualistic
- Flexible unless core values are threatened

**劣势功能（Te）压力反应：**
- Te grip: may become rigid, overly focused on external systems and logistics
- Risk of becoming overly self-critical using external standards against themselves

**发展阶段：**
- Phase I (Childhood): Fi dominant; "highly sensitive persons"; seek refuge in solitary activities (daydreaming, reading, music); develop unique sense of self through Fi and Ne
- Phase II (Adolescence-30s): Ne auxiliary develops; inferior Te tension; oscillate "between unconventional (Fi-Ne) and conventional (Si-Te) paths"; world exploration through travel/countercultures
- Phase III (30s+): "Clearer sense of who they are and how to live authentically and effectively"; learn to routinely transition into Ne perceiving to balance Fi; Te inferior emerges throughout but integrates with maturity

### ENFP

- 来源 URL：https://personalityjunkie.com/the-enfp/ （+ /enfp/ 辅助）
- 功能栈：Ne（主导）- Fi（辅助）- Te（第三）- Si（劣势）
- 覆盖问题：B1, B2, B3, B4, B8, B9

**核心特征：**
- "Enthusiastic, idealistic, restless, and open-minded, with wide-ranging interests"
- Approximately 8% of population, females outnumber males 2:1
- Constantly scanning for new and interesting people, ideas, and possibilities
- "Perhaps more than anything, ENFPs fear boredom and stagnation"
- Value personal experience: "experiencing is believing"
- Among "the least judgmental and most inclusive of all types"

**信息处理：**
- Ne dominant: "restless, anxious" with "eyes commonly darting" searching for "words, ideas, or possibilities"
- Excel at identifying "hidden patterns, possibilities, and potentials" beyond surface data
- Divergent and open-ended: multiplies rather than reduces options
- Brainstorm aloud — exploration that may seem random but serves investigative purpose

**决策方式：**
- Ne-first approach: "approach each situation with openness of Ne; after exploring, use Fi to form a judgment"
- Unlike INFPs who judge first, ENFPs explore first
- Place high value on diverse perspectives; personal experience as primary validator

**对话风格：**
- Talkative and witty with great sense of humor
